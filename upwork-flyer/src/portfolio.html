About me

Design that sells the offer in 3 seconds

I design social media content in Canva every week. I currently manage visual content for a podcast & media client on Upwork: episode announcements, quote posts, news posts and multi-slide carousels, all built on reusable Canva templates and reviewed by the client directly inside a shared Canva folder before anything is published.

For promotional flyers, my focus is simple: one clear offer, one clear action, using your brand colours and assets exactly as provided.
